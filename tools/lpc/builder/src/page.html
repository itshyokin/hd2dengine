About the sprites

Liberated Pixel Cup

Every part comes from the Liberated Pixel Cup (LPC) collection, made by many artists on OpenGameArt.org and gathered by the Universal LPC Spritesheet Character Generator. They're free to use in commercial games as long as the artists are credited.

Licences

  • CC0: no conditions at all.
  • OGA-BY: credit the artists. Fine on Steam and app stores (DRM allowed).
  • CC-BY / CC-BY-SA: credit the artists; they forbid DRM, which makes Steam and app stores unclear, and CC-BY-SA also requires sharing derived sprites under the same licence.
  • GPL: derived sprites must be shared under the GPL too.

A part that lists several licences is available under any one of them. “Steam-safe” means CC0 or OGA-BY is among them. With the switch on, only those parts are offered, and anything not Steam-safe gets an SA/GPL tag.

Weapons

The weapon builder starts from one of the Steam-safe LPC weapons and recolours it part by part (blade, grip, gems…), keeping its shading. A recoloured sprite is still the original artists' work, so their credits travel with it, inside the exported PNG, into the game's Credits screen.

Credits

Each exported PNG carries its credits inside it, and the engine adds them to the game's Credits screen when you import it. The Credits button saves them as a text file too. The game must show credits where players can find them.
